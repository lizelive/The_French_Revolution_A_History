# Realized Ideals

Such is Louis the Beloved, in this year of Grace 1774; such and no other, 
much as courtiers may wish it otherwise. He is getting old, and has ruled for 
sixty years; yet his day is gone, and few regret him. The age of enlightenment 
dawns, though none can yet see what storms it will bring.[3]

Philosophy has conquered; or seems to have conquered. The *Encyclopédie* 
spreads far and wide, carried on wings of commerce and conversation into every 
corner of the kingdom.
